# CORE-EEAT Content Benchmark Reference (80-Item Standard)

The **CORE-EEAT Content Benchmark** provides 8 dimensions × 10 items = 80 evaluation criteria designed to optimize content visibility, citation readiness, and ranking across both modern AI Search Engines (GEO — Generative Engine Optimization) and Traditional Search Engines (SEO).

---

## The Two-System Architecture

| System | Primary Target | Focus Area | Dimensions | Total Items |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CORE** | **GEO** (Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Claude, Google AI Overviews) | Content Body & Semantic Structure | C, O, R, E | 40 items |
| **EEAT** | **SEO** (Google Search, Bing, Search Quality Raters) | Author, Brand Authority, Trust, and Real-World Proof | Exp, Ept, A, T | 40 items |

---

## 1. CORE: Content Body & Optimization (40 Items)

### C — Contextual Clarity & Intent
- **C01 (Intent Alignment)**: The title and headers make a clear promise that the content directly fulfills without clickbait.
- **C02 (Direct Answer)**: Delivers the core answer/definition in the first 150 words (crucial for AI answer engine snippets).
- **C03 (Query Coverage)**: Naturally covers ≥3 related query variations, long-tail questions, and natural-language synonyms.
- **C04 (Definition First)**: Formally defines core concepts and specialized jargon on first introduction.
- **C05 (Topic Scope)**: Clearly establishes what the piece covers and explicitly bounds what is out of scope.
- **C06 (Audience Persona)**: Explicitly states who the guide/article is for and their expected background level.
- **C07 (Semantic Coherence)**: High logical flow between consecutive paragraphs with seamless transitions.
- **C08 (Use Case Decision Tree)**: Includes a decision framework or "When to choose A vs B" guidance.
- **C09 (FAQ Section)**: Includes structured, question-phrased subheadings answering high-frequency reader inquiries.
- **C10 (Semantic Closure)**: Concluding section resolves the opening problem and provides actionable next steps.

### O — Organization & Machine Readability
- **O01 (Strict Hierarchy)**: Proper heading structure (`# H1` → `## H2` → `### H3`) without skipping heading levels.
- **O02 (Executive Summary / TL;DR)**: Prominently features a summary box, key takeaways, or bulleted executive summary.
- **O03 (Structured Data Tables)**: Compares specifications, tools, metrics, or workflows using clean Markdown tables.
- **O04 (List Formatting)**: Uses bulleted and numbered lists for sequential steps, criteria, and parallel items.
- **O05 (Schema Readiness)**: Content structured so JSON-LD (Article, FAQPage, HowTo, TechArticle) can be readily mapped.
- **O06 (Chunking & Cadence)**: Sections focused on a single topic; paragraphs kept to 2–5 sentences for visual breathing room.
- **O07 (Visual Emphasis)**: Boldface used purposefully for key principles, takeaways, and metrics.
- **O08 (Table of Contents)**: Includes quick navigation links for long-form content (>1,500 words).
- **O09 (Information Density)**: Zero repetitive filler; every sentence contributes insight, data, or context.
- **O10 (Multimedia Assets)**: Diagrams (e.g., Mermaid flowcharts, architecture maps) have descriptive captions.

### R — Referenceability & Citation Defense
- **R01 (Data Precision)**: Minimum 5 precise numbers with units (e.g., %, ms, $, benchmarks), avoiding vague "many" or "faster".
- **R02 (Citation Density)**: At least one verifiable reference or external data anchor per 500–750 words.
- **R03 (Source Hierarchy)**: Prioritizes primary sources (original research, documentation, official reports) over secondary commentary.
- **R04 (Evidence Mapping)**: Claims are followed immediately by backing evidence, benchmark data, or direct examples.
- **R05 (Methodology Disclosure)**: Explicitly describes how tests, benchmarks, or workflows were evaluated.
- **R06 (Freshness Timestamp)**: Clearly notes the current version/date of tools, APIs, and market data.
- **R07 (Entity Precision)**: Uses exact canonical names for tools, libraries, organizations, and protocols (no "a certain tool").
- **R08 (Topic Cluster Linking)**: Logically integrates contextual links to related topics and deep dives.
- **R09 (Semantic Markdown/HTML)**: Uses standard semantic elements (`<blockquote>`, `<pre><code>`, `<table>`, `<kbd>`).
- **R10 (Factual Consistency)**: 100% internal consistency—no contradictory statements or misaligned figures across sections.

### E — Exclusivity & Information Gain
- **E01 (Original Data/Synthesis)**: Features synthesized insights, novel comparisons, or firsthand analysis not copy-pasted elsewhere.
- **E02 (Novel Conceptual Framework)**: Introduces a named, citable framework, mental model, or rubric.
- **E03 (Primary Research / Walkthrough)**: Walks through actual implementations, code snippets, or real configurations.
- **E04 (Nuanced / Contrarian Analysis)**: Directly addresses consensus myths, edge cases, trade-offs, and where solutions fail.
- **E05 (Original Visual Diagrams)**: Includes custom Mermaid diagrams, architecture flows, or decision flowcharts.
- **E06 (Competitor Gap Filling)**: Explicitly answers tricky sub-questions that competing articles ignore.
- **E07 (Actionable Artifacts)**: Provides copy-pasteable code, config templates, checklists, or step-by-step guides.
- **E08 (Comprehensive Depth)**: Thoroughly explores edge cases, failure modes, and performance characteristics.
- **E09 (Cross-Domain Synthesis)**: Connects insights across related domains (e.g., developer ergonomics + infrastructure costs).
- **E10 (Forward-Looking Perspective)**: Offers concrete, reasoned projections on where the ecosystem is heading.

## 2. EEAT: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness & Trust (40 Items)

### Exp — Experience
- Real-world practitioner perspective ("In production environments, we observed...").
- Hard-won lessons, failure modes, and troubleshooting tips.
- Concrete benchmark setup and execution context.

### Ept — Expertise
- Deep technical fluency and accurate terminology without buzzword salad.
- Explaining the *why* behind architectural and design decisions.
- Accurate representation of underlying mechanics, APIs, and protocols.

### A — Authoritativeness
- Citing authoritative standards, RFCs, official docs, and primary research.
- Clear alignment with industry best practices and consensus standards.
- Citable entity relationships and structured metadata.

### T — Trust
- Unbiased evaluation: presenting drawbacks, limitations, and costs openly.
- Clear disclosures, accurate timestamps, and verifiable external citations.
- Absence of hyperbolic sales pitches, affiliate stuffing, or manipulated benchmark claims.
