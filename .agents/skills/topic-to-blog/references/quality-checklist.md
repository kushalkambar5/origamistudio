# Topic-to-Blog Pre-Publish Quality Checklist

Run this quick audit on the drafted article before presenting the final output to the user.

---

## 1. Deep Research & Evidence Verification
- [ ] **Multi-Angle Coverage**: Does the article cover facts, real-world examples, expert consensus/debates, and forward trends?
- [ ] **Data Precision**: Are there at least 3–5 specific quantitative metrics with units (%, ms, $, sample sizes, dates)?
- [ ] **Entity Precision**: Are tools, libraries, protocols, and companies referred to by their exact, accurate names?
- [ ] **Source Integrity**: Are claims backed by citable authorities or verifiable technical mechanisms?

---

## 2. SEO & GEO (Search & AI Engine Optimization)
- [ ] **Direct Answer Block**: Is there a clear, high-density direct answer in the first 150 words?
- [ ] **Heading Structure**: Does the document follow a clean `# H1` → `## H2` → `### H3` hierarchy without skipping levels?
- [ ] **Structured Tables**: Is there at least one Markdown comparison or specification table?
- [ ] **FAQ Section**: Are common reader questions addressed using conversational, question-phrased subheadings?
- [ ] **Metadata**: Are Title Tag and Meta Description (140–160 characters) provided?

---

## 3. Humanizer & Voice Quality (Zero AI Slop)
- [ ] **No Inflation Tropes**: Zero occurrences of `"stands as a testament"`, `"pivotal moment"`, `"in today's digital age"`, `"underscores the importance"`.
- [ ] **No Trailing `-ing` Clauses**: No sentences lazily ending in `, highlighting...`, `, underscoring...`, `, showcasing...`.
- [ ] **No Banned Vocabulary**: Free of `delve`, `utilize`, `transformative`, `plethora`, `beacon`, `tapestry`, `groundbreaking`.
- [ ] **No Weasel Attributions**: No unbacked `"industry experts agree"` or `"studies show"`.
- [ ] **Cadence & Rhythm**: Sentence lengths are varied with natural conversational rhythm and momentum.
- [ ] **Grounded Opinions**: The article takes a stance, acknowledges trade-offs, and embraces real-world nuances.

---

## 4. Multi-Format & Visual Assets
- [ ] **Visual Diagrams**: Architecture, workflows, or mental models visualized using clean Mermaid blocks.
- [ ] **Code Formatting**: Code snippets specify proper language syntax highlighting and realistic, runnable logic.
- [ ] **Actionable Takeaways**: Clear, concrete next steps for the reader.
