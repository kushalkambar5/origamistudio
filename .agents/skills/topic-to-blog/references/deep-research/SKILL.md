# Deep Research Skill

A systematic methodology for conducting thorough, high-conviction web research. **Always execute this research process BEFORE starting any content generation or drafting task** to ensure you gather comprehensive information from multiple angles, depths, and verified sources.

## Core Principle

**Never generate substantive content based solely on shallow knowledge or a single search query.** The quality, authority, and value of your output directly depends on the breadth, depth, and precision of research conducted beforehand. A single search query is never enough.

---

## When to Use This Skill

Activate this skill whenever:
- **Research Requests**: Investigating a complex concept, technology, industry trend, or market landscape.
- **Pre-Writing & Content Synthesis**: Writing articles, blog posts, whitepapers, benchmarks, or documentation requiring real-world evidence, statistics, and citations.
- **Comparative Analysis**: Evaluating trade-offs between tools, frameworks, paradigms, or architectural choices.

---

## The 4-Phase Research Methodology

### Phase 1: Broad Exploration (Landscape Mapping)

Start with wide-aperture searches to survey the entire domain and identify critical dimensions:

1. **Initial Survey**: Search the core topic to understand background, recent developments, and main players.
2. **Identify Dimensions**: From initial findings, isolate 3–6 key subtopics, architectural layers, or thematic angles.
3. **Map Perspectives**: Note differing viewpoints, controversies, consensus areas, and competing methodologies.

*Example Dimension Mapping:*
```
Topic: "Agentic AI in Enterprise Software Development"
Identified Dimensions:
- 1. Architecture: Single-agent vs. multi-agent orchestration, tool-use protocols (MCP)
- 2. Evaluation: Benchmarking benchmarks (SWE-bench), real-world pass rates vs. synthetic tests
- 3. Security & Governance: Sandboxing, permission models, prompt injection defenses
- 4. Economic Impact: Developer productivity metrics, token economics, ROI case studies
- 5. Industry Adoption: Production case studies, organizational hurdles, migration patterns
```

---

### Phase 2: Targeted Deep Dive (Dimension Drilldown)

For each identified dimension, conduct targeted searches to extract granular information:

1. **Specific Queries**: Use precise, boolean-style search queries.
2. **Multiple Phrasings**: Rephrase technical terms and jargon to find both practitioner discussions and academic/industry reports.
3. **Fetch Full Content**: Read full documentation, whitepapers, or technical articles rather than relying solely on search snippets.
4. **Follow Citations**: Trace references to foundational sources, original research papers, or GitHub repositories.

---

### Phase 3: Diversity & Validation Across 4 Information Types

To ensure comprehensive and unbiased coverage, systematically gather each of the four core information types:

| Information Type | Purpose | Targeted Query Modifiers | Output Deliverable |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Facts & Data** | Concrete evidence & empirical metrics | `"statistics"`, `"data"`, `"benchmarks"`, `"numbers"`, `"market size"`, `"latency ms"` | Exact metrics with units, sample sizes, and dates |
| **2. Examples & Case Studies** | Real-world validation & practical mechanics | `"case study"`, `"production deployment"`, `"real-world implementation"`, `"post-mortem"` | Concrete company/project examples and workflows |
| **3. Expert Opinions & Debates** | Industry perspectives & nuanced friction points | `"interview"`, `"analysis"`, `"criticism"`, `"trade-offs"`, `"engineering blog"` | Attributed quotes, consensus views, and contrarian perspectives |
| **4. Trends & Projections** | Forward-looking direction & next milestones | `"roadmap"`, `"future of"`, `"next generation"`, `"emerging patterns"` | 12-to-36-month horizon insights backed by current trajectories |

---

### Phase 4: Synthesis Check (Quality Gate)

Before writing any content or concluding research, run this verification checklist:

- [ ] **Breadth**: Did you investigate at least 3–5 distinct dimensions or subtopics?
- [ ] **Completeness**: Did you fetch and analyze primary sources or in-depth materials for critical claims?
- [ ] **Data Precision**: Do you have specific figures, percentages, dates, and named entities (not vague generalizations)?
- [ ] **Balanced Viewpoint**: Did you capture both advantages/successes and limitations/trade-offs/risks?
- [ ] **Freshness & Source Authority**: Are the sources credible, authoritative, and reflective of the current state?

> **Rule**: If any check above fails, run targeted follow-up searches to close the gap before moving to drafting.
